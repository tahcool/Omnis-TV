Omnis Auxiliary Screens

Wall screens: Daily Breakdowns, Work in Progress, Major Defects, Service Tracking, Live Dispatch and Service Bookings.

1Display PIN

Enter the display PIN from your Omnis administrator. It is stored encrypted on this PC.

3Start-up

Recommended for wall screens: the TVs come back by themselves after a power cut or restart.

2Screens

Choose what each monitor shows. A monitor can show two screens, switching between them (every 60 seconds unless you change it). Use Identify monitors to see which number is which TV, and leave the PC's own screen on Nothing if it is not a TV.

On a TV: Ctrl+Shift+S opens this setup  ·  Ctrl+Shift+Q closes the screens
